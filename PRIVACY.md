# Privacy Policy — Mantra Counter

**App package:** `com.starborn.mantracounter`
**Effective date:** 11 August 2026 · **Last updated:** 11 August 2026

Mantra Counter is a japa counter that keeps everything on your own phone. The app does **not**
collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has **no internet permission at all**, so
it is technically incapable of sending your information anywhere.

## Data we collect

**None.** Mantra Counter has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no
third-party SDKs. We do not gather your name, email, location, device identifiers, or usage
statistics.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in the app is stored **locally on your device only**, in the app's private
storage. This includes:

- Your mantra names, deity labels, favourites, and settings
- Your counts, completed malas, lifetime targets, and day-by-day history
- Any background photo you choose for a mantra
- App preferences such as theme, mala size, and sound or vibration settings

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it yourself (see below).
Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions

The app requests a single permission: **Vibrate**, used only to give a short buzz when you
complete a mala. It requests **no internet or network permission**, no location, no contacts, and
no microphone or camera access.

Choosing a background photo and saving a backup file use Android's built-in system pickers, which
do *not* grant the app broad access to your storage — the app only receives the one file you pick.

## Backups you create

The app can export a plain-text backup of your mantras and history to a file location you choose.
After it is saved, Android may offer to share that file (for example by email, WhatsApp, or
Telegram). Any such sharing is **initiated entirely by you**, to a destination you choose. Once a
backup file leaves the app, it is under your control and governed by the privacy terms of wherever
you send or store it.

## Children

Mantra Counter does not collect any data from anyone, including children, and is safe for users of
all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new
"Last updated" date.
